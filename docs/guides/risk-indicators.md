# Risk Indicators and Abuse Patterns

This document replaces marketing-oriented material with defensive documentation for identifying token-abuse patterns.

## Common Risk Indicators

- Claims of "undetectable" or "scanner-proof" honeypots.
- Owner-controlled switches affecting sellability.
- Blacklist/whitelist controls over transfers.
- Hidden or obfuscated owner checks.
- Aggressive promotion with promises of guaranteed profit.

## Defensive Guidance

- Treat marketing claims as adversarial.
- Require code review and fork-based transaction simulation.
- Verify contract ownership, privileged functions, and liquidity controls.
- Prefer audited, transparent contracts with minimized privileges.
